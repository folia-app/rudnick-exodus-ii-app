ABOUT MODAL

EXODUS 2 is a series of 19 pangram haikus
composed by David Rudnick between 2010 and 2011.

Each work is a haiku which, as a pangram, contains all 26 letters of the standard western alphabet.

The works are locked in a custom smart contract in an inverse fibionacci sequence, with the final work unlocked 40 years from the first.

Once unlocked, a 24h auction sequence is triggered with no reserve. New high bids in the last 15 minutes extend the auction timer by a further 15 minutes until close.
